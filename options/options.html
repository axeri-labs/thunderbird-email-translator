Email Translator

Target language
The language emails are translated into.
Translation engine
Google Translate — free, no account and no key, detects the source language automatically.
MyMemory — free, no account. Limited per IP address: about 10 requests a minute and 1,000 words a day.
DeepL — best quality, 500,000 characters a month free. Needs a free API key: deepl.com
MyMemory cannot detect the source language, so set the language your emails are written in. It has to differ from the target language, otherwise MyMemory refuses the request. Google Translate and DeepL detect it on their own.
Not a login and not a registration: MyMemory only uses the address to identify the quota, and leaving it empty is fine. Filling it in raises the daily limit from 1,000 to 10,000 words per IP address.
Get a free key at deepl.com/pro-api — no credit card required.
